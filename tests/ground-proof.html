The Learn grounds against what the reader can actually see

Cream block = the reading column, opaque, covers everything behind it. Gold strip = the navigation, full height, last item ends at unit 360. Terracotta block = the right rail, which is position: sticky and therefore covers the same band at every scroll position. Its depth is PER COURSE — these are measured, not assumed, and an earlier version of this page drew Acting's 287 for all four, which is how a dressing mirror and a whole monitor came to be drawn where nobody could see them.
