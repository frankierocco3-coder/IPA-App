# Articulation artwork — the remaining 18

44 of 62 sounds have drawings. These 18 do not, and currently fall back to the
generated diagram in `js/diagram.js`, which looks nothing like the hand-drawn set.

Same conventions as the delivered pack: 4:5 portrait, IPA symbol in the header,
coral marks for airflow / release / movement / contact, dark curved marks at the
throat for voicing. Numbering continues from 047.

## Why these cannot reuse an existing drawing

Every one of these is an accent-specific sound, and the accent courses exist
*because* it differs from the RP equivalent. Australian FACE is not RP FACE with
a different label — it starts from a visibly more open mouth. Putting the /eɪ/
drawing on /æɪ/ would teach the wrong thing. So each brief below names the
nearest already-drawn sound and states exactly what the new drawing must show
differently. That contrast IS the teaching.

## The briefs

### /ɛː/ — contemporary SQUARE

- **File:** `048-vowel-epsilon-long.png`
- **Words:** square, fair, there
- **Phonetics:** a long steady open-mid front vowel — where older RP glided /eə/, modern British holds one position
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /eə/ (`014-diphthong-e-schwa.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** RP glides from /e/ toward schwa. This one does not move: one steady tongue position, held long. Draw the mouth static, no glide arrow.

### /i/ — happY vowel

- **File:** `049-vowel-happy.png`
- **Words:** happy, city, duty
- **Phonetics:** the weak-but-tense vowel ending happy, city, coffee — /iː/ quality without its length, only in unstressed positions
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /iː/ (`015-vowel-long-i.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Same tongue shape as FLEECE, no length. Show it slightly less extreme than /iː/ and mark it as unstressed-only.

### /ʔ/ — glottal stop

- **File:** `050-consonant-glottal-stop.png`
- **Words:** better, water, got
- **Phonetics:** a spoken realization [ʔ] of the phoneme /t/, not an extra consonant — the vocal folds close and release. Common before consonants, at word ends and before unstressed syllables: /ˈbetə/ → [ˈbeʔə]. Use varies by speaker, setting and environment; careful speech keeps [t]
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /t/ (`042-consonant-t.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Nothing happens in the mouth. The vocal folds themselves close and release. The tongue and lips stay wherever the surrounding sounds left them; all the action is at the larynx.

### /ɑ/ — American LOT/PALM

- **File:** `051-vowel-script-a-american.png`
- **Words:** lot, father, stop
- **Phonetics:** open back unrounded vowel — the unrounded American “ah”
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ɑː/ (`018-vowel-long-script-a.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Open back, unrounded. Show the lips clearly NOT rounded, to separate it from THOUGHT.

### /oʊ/ — American GOAT

- **File:** `052-diphthong-o-upsilon.png`
- **Words:** go, show, soap
- **Phonetics:** glides from mid back rounded toward close back
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /əʊ/ (`010-diphthong-schwa-upsilon.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Starts at a rounded mid-back vowel, not at schwa. The lips are already rounded at the START.

### /ɝ/ — American NURSE

- **File:** `053-vowel-rhotic-nurse.png`
- **Words:** nurse, word, learn
- **Phonetics:** r-colored mid central vowel (stressed)
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ɜː/ (`025-vowel-long-reversed-e.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** NURSE plus r-colouring. The tongue bunches up or the tip curls back while the vowel sounds. That bunching is the whole point of the drawing.

### /ɚ/ — American lettER

- **File:** `054-vowel-rhotic-letter.png`
- **Words:** teacher, father, doctor
- **Phonetics:** r-colored schwa (unstressed)
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ə/ (`026-vowel-schwa.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Schwa plus the same r-colouring as /ɝ/, but unstressed and shorter.

### /ɐ/ — Australian STRUT

- **File:** `055-vowel-turned-a.png`
- **Words:** cup, but, love
- **Phonetics:** open central vowel — the Australian “uh”
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ʌ/ (`020-vowel-turned-v.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** More open and more central than RP STRUT.

### /ɐː/ — Australian PALM/BATH

- **File:** `056-vowel-turned-a-long.png`
- **Words:** palm, father, start
- **Phonetics:** long open central vowel
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ɑː/ (`018-vowel-long-script-a.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Central, not back. Show the tongue further forward than the RP PALM drawing.

### /ɔ/ — Australian LOT

- **File:** `057-vowel-open-o-aus.png`
- **Words:** lot, odd, wash
- **Phonetics:** short open-mid back rounded vowel — sits higher than the RP /ɒ/
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ɒ/ (`023-vowel-turned-script-a.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Sits HIGHER than RP LOT, and rounded.

### /oː/ — Australian THOUGHT

- **File:** `058-vowel-o-long-aus.png`
- **Words:** thought, law, north
- **Phonetics:** long close-mid back rounded vowel — noticeably higher than RP /ɔː/
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ɔː/ (`022-vowel-long-open-o.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Noticeably higher and tighter-rounded than RP THOUGHT.

### /eː/ — Australian SQUARE

- **File:** `059-vowel-e-long-aus.png`
- **Words:** square, fair, there
- **Phonetics:** long mid front vowel — a steady monophthong where RP glides /eə/
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /eə/ (`014-diphthong-e-schwa.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** A steady monophthong where RP glides. No glide arrow.

### /æɔ/ — Australian MOUTH

- **File:** `060-diphthong-ash-open-o.png`
- **Words:** mouth, now, out
- **Phonetics:** glides from near-open front toward rounded back
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /aʊ/ (`011-diphthong-a-upsilon.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Starts near-open FRONT (the TRAP position), not central, and ends rounded back.

### /æɪ/ — Australian FACE

- **File:** `061-diphthong-ash-small-cap-i.png`
- **Words:** face, day, rain
- **Phonetics:** glides from near-open front toward close front
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /eɪ/ (`013-diphthong-e-small-cap-i.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Starts much more OPEN than RP FACE — from TRAP, not DRESS.

### /ɑe/ — Australian PRICE

- **File:** `062-diphthong-script-a-e.png`
- **Words:** price, high, try
- **Phonetics:** glides from open back toward a mid front vowel — a wide, open journey
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /aɪ/ (`008-diphthong-a-small-cap-i.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Starts open BACK and ends mid front. A wider journey across the mouth than RP PRICE.

### /oɪ/ — Australian CHOICE

- **File:** `063-diphthong-o-small-cap-i.png`
- **Words:** choice, boy, noise
- **Phonetics:** glides from a close-mid back rounded start toward close front — starts higher than RP /ɔɪ/
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /ɔɪ/ (`009-diphthong-open-o-small-cap-i.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Starts HIGHER than RP CHOICE — close-mid back rounded.

### /əʉ/ — Australian GOAT

- **File:** `064-diphthong-schwa-barred-u.png`
- **Words:** goat, show, no
- **Phonetics:** glides from schwa toward a fronted close rounded vowel
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /əʊ/ (`010-diphthong-schwa-upsilon.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Ends at a FRONTED close rounded vowel, not a back one. The lips round while the tongue moves forward.

### /ʉː/ — Australian GOOSE

- **File:** `065-vowel-barred-u.png`
- **Words:** goose, two, blue
- **Phonetics:** long close central rounded vowel — fronted “oo”
- **Nearest drawn sound:** /uː/ (`027-vowel-long-u.jpg`)
- **Must show differently:** Central and fronted, not back. Rounded lips but the tongue is much further forward than RP GOOSE.
