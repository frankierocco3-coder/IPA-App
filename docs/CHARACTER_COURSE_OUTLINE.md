# Building a Character: course outline (draft for owner approval)

Drafted 2026-09-22, restructured twice the same day on owner direction. Nothing
here is built yet. Approve, cut or reorder, and lessons get written
module by module.

**The organising idea (owner, 2026-09-22):** character work is three
different jobs.
1. **From scratch:** you invent the person yourself, from your own
   experience and observation, the way solo and devised work does.
2. **A given character:** you've been cast in a role a writer created, and
   you build that person from the script.
3. **Commedia dell'Arte:** the tradition created the person, with every
   trait already fixed (Arlecchino has his walk, mask, status, hunger,
   voice and bits before you arrive). You learn the form exactly, then
   find your own life inside it.

All three share one toolkit. The course does NOT frame character work as
"two roads" (analysis vs feeling). Every source it draws on has its own
method, and all of them serve all three jobs.

**Structure:** Foundations, then the Toolkit, then the three tracks.
Learners can open any module from the Library in any order; the Learn path
is just the recommended order.

**What it is:** a fourth workspace beside Acting, Voice & Speech and Accents &
Dialects, on Acting's record system (Learn path, Practice shelf, Library;
every collection maps to exactly one module).
**How it launches:** it's built behind a `CHARACTER_LIVE` switch, the same
mechanism as `SPEECH_LIVE`, and turns on once it's substantial enough to
stand alone.
**The existing Acting lessons (owner, 2026-09-22):** Acting is left alone
while this course is built. Once it's built, ALL of Acting's Module 4
("Building a Character", nine lessons) comes OUT of Acting, because this
course covers all of it and more. The eight lessons this outline marks
*(from Acting)* MOVE here at that point; Acting 4.1 (the two-ways
framing) is retired, not moved. They keep their lesson ids, so anyone who
already completed them keeps that progress, and no stored data needs
migrating. Acting's later modules renumber, and any Acting page that
links into Module 4 gets repointed. Until the move, this course shows
them through the existing `sharedFrom` mechanism.

Totals: 5 parts, 9 modules, 54 lessons (8 from Acting, 46 new).

## Part 1: Commedia dell’Arte (the way in)

Commedia comes FIRST (owner decision 2026-09-22). Its characters arrive
complete, with every part of a character visible from the outside, so
studying them teaches what any character is made of before the learner
builds their own. The course opens with "Why We Start With Commedia", and
the same breakdown later becomes the My Characters worksheet.

### Module 1: Commedia dell’Arte (9 lessons + 9 reference chapters)

**The path (9):** Why We Start With Commedia, The History of Commedia,
Slapstick, The Scenario, Learning a Form, What Is an Archetype, The Mask
Leads, The Cast, Lazzi.

**First and Second Zanni is gone as a lesson** (owner order 2026-09-22):
the first-zanni job is taught in Brighella's chapter, the second-zanni
job and the frightened zanni run in Arlecchino's, and The Cast names the
split where it introduces the servants.

**The Cast** (1.8) meets the whole company: one card per principal
character with family, city, want and contradiction, rostered from the
mask records, plus the wider company (24 more names, from Scapino to
Tartaglia, under the note that there is no official list).

**The nine character chapters are REFERENCE pages** (`reference: true`),
reached by tapping a card in The Cast. They are not lessons, not on the
Library shelf and not counted in progress, and they keep everything in
them: the sheet, relationships, the full breakdown, the picture slots,
the way in and where to see them (owner order 2026-09-22). The Masks at a
Glance shelves at the end of the Commedia chapters as the one-page
reference.

## Two more Bogosian lessons: ADDED 2026-09-22

From the owner's copy of the essay, two things it describes that the
Acting lessons never covered, both in Making the Piece:

* **Everyday Performances** (7.3): behaviour in front of other people is
  always performance, so collect the real ones (a teacher, a preacher, a
  trainer, a toast, a safety briefing) and improvise inside them. The
  situation supplies a reason to talk, a captive audience and a public
  manner.
* **Questions the Improv Missed** (7.7): the plain questions improvising
  never reaches. What was this person doing ten minutes ago, what are
  they wearing and carrying, how old are they and what does that do to
  the voice and posture, indoors or out, who else is there, what happens
  after. Answers jog the imagination; nothing is scored.

## Studio: My Characters (BUILT 2026-09-22)

The workspace's Studio is a shelf of the characters the learner is
building, each a worksheet with the same breakdown the commedia chapters
use. Three kinds, matching the three tracks: from scratch (adds the
people observed), a role you have been cast in (adds the given
circumstances and what others say, linked to a script), and a commedia
mask (the tradition's answer beside every field). This IS the Character
Bible and the Observation Notebook from the plan below; they are no
longer separate items.

## Practice (written guidance, no audio needed)

Each deals cards or prompts, the way the Arcade already does. Nothing is
scored.

* **Walk Builder** (Toolkit): deals a centre, a tempo and a weight to walk.
* **Status Cards** (Toolkit): deals a status number to each person in a scene.
* **Mask Cards** (Commedia): deals a commedia type, a want and a lazzo.
* **Observation Notebook** (From Scratch): a prompt sheet saved to a Studio project.
* **Character Bible** (Given): the 8.4 template, saved to a Studio project, using a script from Scenes or Monologues or your own.
* **The Warmup:** shared from Acting Practice, unchanged.
